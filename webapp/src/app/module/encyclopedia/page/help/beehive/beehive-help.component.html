Poppyopedia » Glossary » Beehive

The Beehive is an add-on you can get for your house. Its bees head out to the hive's spaces to collect things, and they head out more often when fed with floral, fruity, and planty foods.

The Hive's Spaces

Every Beehive has a handful of spaces around it - jungle, beach, grassy, and rocky - and each kind of terrain has its own sort of stuff lying around for bees to pick up.

Different bees do different things with a space:

  • Worker bees collect the space's stuff. Normally.
  • Hard-worker bees collect more of the space's stuff than usual.
  • Royalty bees ignore the space's stuff and bring back Royal Jelly instead. (They're very focused.)
  • A helper pet hunts or gathers for whatever it can find, and brings that back instead.

Flower Power

Flower Power is a kind of fuel for your beehive. It's filled by feeding the beehive, and keeps the bees energetic - the more they have, the sooner they're ready to head out and collect.

The more bees in your Beehive, the faster they work, but also the more items you'll have to feed them!

More?!

As you build up your household, the Beehive will gain new powers. You'll just have to play to find out what they are!
