Poppyopedia » Glossary » ★Kindred

★Kindred is a tabletop game you play with your pets. Each pet plays a fantasy hero - with a race, a class, stats, and skills - and you play the story-teller, sending them on adventures and doling out rewards.

Characters

  • Before a pet can play, it needs a character. Characters are rolled completely at random: race, class, name, portrait, and stats.
  • A character's class decides which stats grow fastest, and which skills are its specialties (its "class skills"). Class skills improve every level; other skills improve half as quickly.
  • Your pets may have up to 10 active (un-retired) characters at a time.

Adventures

  • Every day, there are two new adventures to choose from - the same two for everyone. Each tests different skills.
  • You may go on one adventure per day, with a party of up to three adventurers.
  • Choose a difficulty: Novice, Veteran, Hero, or Demigod. Every adventure lists four rewards, one per difficulty; winning earns the reward for the difficulty you chose, and every easier one. Every adventurer in the party raises the bar for the whole party, so bring the characters whose skills fit the adventure!
  • Some adventures offer a hat styling as their Hero reward. (If you already have it, you won't get it again - but the Demigod reward is always an item.)
  • Win or lose, every adventurer earns experience. Winning earns a lot more.

Retirement

  • Level 20 adventurers can only go on one more adventure: The Final Adventure, which retires them. (Adventurers retire together, so every member of that party must be level 20.)
  • Retired adventurers are remembered forever in the Book of Retired Adventurers, and the pet who played them is free to roll up a new character.
